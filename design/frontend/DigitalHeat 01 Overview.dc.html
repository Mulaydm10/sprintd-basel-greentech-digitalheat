Sense. Price. Dispatch.

Sense

Each house posts its heat demand and tank level. Demand follows the case formula on Basel 2025 weather; hot water adds 12 kWh a day.

02CHF per kWh
heat dumped · 0.3325✕ ballast
bid 0.25
heat used · ~0.226✓ runs

Price

Each kWh costs 0.3325 CHF. When the heat is used it is worth price / 2.5, so the net cost drops to about 0.226. Cheap flexible work only clears where its heat is used.

03{{ g3State }}

Dispatch

Jobs run in 15-minute chunks. When the house is warm they checkpoint and pause, then resume at the next chunk here or move to another eligible house.
