01 · The core idea

We gave every kilowatt-hour a price.

Every hour each house says what a kWh of compute costs it. It's cheaper when the heat warms the house and dearer when it would be dumped. Work runs where its price clears. One function, decide(), drives both the 8,760-hour simulation and the live machines.

03 · Where does a job clear? Try it

Pick a job. Click a house to change its weather.

A house's cost is 0.226 CHF/kWh when its heat is used and 0.3325 when it would be dumped. A job runs where its bid covers the cost and its access class allows the site. A 1 kW server burns 1 kWh an hour, so CHF per server-hour and CHF per kWh compare directly.

04 · Patience is a battery

Jobs that can wait, wait for the cold.

Slack is the time a job may wait before its deadline. The more slack, the more work moves out of warm afternoons into cold nights, where its heat is wanted.
