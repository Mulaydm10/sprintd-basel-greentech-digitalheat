Before you join

  • Your phone will get warm. If you become a server it runs a heavy compute job (CPU + graphics) and heats up on purpose: that heat is the product.
  • Tap STOP any time, for example when it feels warm. The job then moves to another phone, without losing its progress.
  • Keep this page open and the screen on. Servers: please turn the brightness to maximum and plug in if you can.
  • It uses some battery (a few %). Nothing is installed; closing the page stops everything.
  • No personal data: you get an anonymous volunteer number. Don't use this if your phone is already hot or below 20 % battery.
